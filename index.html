Hostel Rules

1

Entry & Exit: Return by 9:30 PM; late entry requires warden permission.

2

Visitors: Visitors are allowed only in the reception area between 5:00-7:00 PM.

3

Silence Hours: Keep noise low from 10:00 PM to 6:00 AM.

4

Cleanliness: Keep rooms, bathrooms and common areas clean.

5

Restricted Items: Smoking, alcohol and illegal substances are strictly prohibited.

6

ID Card: Carry your hostel ID card at all times.

7

Damage: Students must pay for any damage they cause to hostel property.

8

Appliances: High-power cooking and heating appliances are not allowed in rooms.
